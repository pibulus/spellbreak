I lose track of time pretty badly. Coding, gaming, down a rabbit hole, and suddenly it's dark outside and I haven't eaten.

I needed something sweet but stern to break the spell, so I made Spellbreak for Mac: a small menu bar app that washes every screen in soft moving light for 20 seconds so I remember to drink my tea while it's hot, stretch my back, and feed the cat.

A few small details I care about:
- Hold the ring for 2-3 seconds to leave early, or turn on Lock mode so the break runs its full clock with no way to skip.
- Automatically waits during calls (Zoom, Meet, Slack, Discord) without ever touching or recording audio.
- Zero network access, no accounts, no streaks, no subscriptions. Sandboxed and notarized for macOS 13+ (Apple silicon & Intel).

It's live now at https://spellbreak.app (~US$20 / A$29 once), and you can test the break right in the browser on the home page.

If you give it a spin through a real afternoon of work, I'd love to hear how the timing and friction feel.

Cheers! -P

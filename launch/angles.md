## Concrete details

Hook: Full-screen Aurora, Ember, and Violet breaks across every display, call-aware via Core Audio, zero network access, notarized for macOS 13+.
Use when: Directories, changelogs, or developer communities (`r/macapps`, HN) that want technical clarity immediately.
Why it lands: Specific facts instead of slogans.
Social lead:
- 20-second breaks every 20 minutes by default (10s to 3m breaks, 15m to 3h intervals).
- 2-3 second hold to leave early, or Lock mode with no skip ring.
- Waits automatically during calls and active typing.
- Zero network access: sandboxed, Developer ID signed, and notarized.
